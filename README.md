# 🍽️ Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard | MS Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales, orders, customer ratings, food categories, restaurant performance, and regional sales.

The dashboard converts raw food-delivery data into meaningful business insights that can help stakeholders monitor performance, understand customer demand, and identify areas for business improvement.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A food-delivery business generates large amounts of sales, order, restaurant, and customer-rating data. Analyzing this data manually can make it difficult to identify important trends and performance gaps.

This dashboard was developed to answer key business questions such as:

- How are sales changing over time?
- Which days and months generate higher sales?
- Which food categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which locations generate higher sales?
- Which restaurants have stronger sales performance?
- How many orders are being generated?
- What is the average customer rating?
- How does performance vary across quarters?

### 💡 Business Objective

The objective is to provide a **centralized and interactive Excel dashboard** that helps stakeholders monitor sales performance, understand customer demand, compare regions and restaurants, and support data-driven business decisions.

## 📈 Key Analysis

### Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed monthly sales trends to identify changes in revenue and periods of higher or lower sales.

**Business use:** Helps management monitor sales trends and identify periods that may require additional promotional or operational strategies.

### Daily Sales Analysis

Compared sales performance across different days of the week.

**Business use:** Helps identify high-demand days and supports planning for restaurant operations and delivery capacity.

### Food Category Analysis

Compared sales across different food categories.

**Business use:** Helps understand customer preferences and supports decisions related to menu offerings and category-level promotions.

### Regional Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across different cities/regions.

**Business use:** Helps identify strong and weak markets and supports regional marketing and expansion decisions.

### Restaurant Analysis

Compared performance across restaurants.

**Business use:** Helps identify restaurants contributing significantly to overall sales and provides visibility into restaurant-level performance.

### Quarterly Analysis

Compared sales, orders, and ratings across quarters.

**Business use:** Helps management evaluate performance over different periods and identify changes in customer demand.
